## Evidence required during implementation

Attach automated test results, bounded lab logs, browser checks, and measured performance results to the corresponding plan/tasks. Security-sensitive scenarios need both accepted and rejected cases. Test fixtures may establish behavior but do not establish compatibility with a real provider version; record both separately. No release acceptance is implied by completing the specification-quality checklist.
